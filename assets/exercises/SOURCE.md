Exercise thumbnails are derived from yuhonas/free-exercise-db:
https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db

Source images are distributed under the Unlicense (see LICENSE.md).
Atlas includes the first reference image for each of the 873 bundled entries,
resized to at most 192 pixels and encoded as WebP for offline display.

Regenerate with `node scripts/import-exercise-thumbnails.mjs` after installing
the landing page dependencies. Metadata-only expansion entries do not supply
licensed reference photographs and are not represented as photographed moves.
